# G1: Every Bullet a Tracer — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make every round in the game a tracer in its team's colour, drawn with a white-hot core in a coloured glow, a burning head, a speed-length streak, embers where rounds strike and glancing ricochets.

**Architecture:**
- **Colour:** the session takes the player's tracer kind from a new `Session.team`. Other players' rounds take their seat's team in `Together.prepare`. The loadout's tracer choice and `Gear.tracer` are deleted.
- **Drawing:** ribbons gain a style word, so the effects shader draws a tracer's core and glow in one instance, while the flash star keeps its old profile. Each tracer also gets an emissive head billboard.
- **Embers and ricochets** are cosmetic, in the app layer (`src/app/ricochets.ts`), fed by impacts that now carry their direction and kind.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, raw WebGPU (WGSL), Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-trenchline-shots-design.md` §1 (read it; this plan argues from it).

## Global Constraints

- **Determinism:** `src/core`, `src/world`, `src/sim`, `src/combat`, `src/battle` and `src/net` follow `tests/architecture.test.ts`. They use `core/dmath` instead of `Math.sin`/`cos`/`exp`/`sqrt`/`floor`, and may not touch the DOM, WebGPU, WebAudio, time or `crypto`. `src/app`, `src/render` and `src/ui` may.
- **Tunables:** every number lives in `data/tunables.toml` with a comment, and is read through `Tunables`. No magic numbers in logic.
- **Colours:** red `[1.0, 0.16, 0.10]`, blue `[0.20, 0.50, 1.0]`, amber `[1.0, 0.83, 0.45]`.
- **TRACER_KIND** (`src/net/gear.ts`): `{ none: 0, amber: 1, red: 2, blue: 3 }`. The pool stores kinds 0–3, and the shot word's 2 bits carry them.
- **Rooms:** builds must match (`net/room-core.ts` refuses another), so no wire compatibility is owed to older builds. The protocol (`PROTOCOL = 1`) does not change, and the relay needs no change.
- **Perf:** the both-peaks gate (`tests/e2e/peak.spec.ts`) holds GPU p95 ≤ 14 ms on this Mac in Chrome.
- **Commits:** end every commit message with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work on branch `shots`. Never push.
- **Quiet tests:** run unit tests with `npx vitest run <files>` and e2e with `npx playwright test <spec>`. No other e2e run may be going (they share ports 5320/8787 and the GPU).

## Review Focus

- **Aiming down the sights on full auto:** every round is a tracer, so a stream leaves the muzzle under the sight line. The first 4 m must be lit in (ignite) and the head drawn only from 1.5 m, or the sight picture is buried in light. Task 2's `tracer ignites over its first metres` test pins it.
- **A round passing within a metre of the eye:** the front's heavy fire aims at the head. The glow must be capped at `tracer_glow_max_px`, or it draws a bar across the screen. Task 2's shader capture test takes a ribbon 0.5 m from the eye.
- **Full pool:** 256 rounds plus 900 battle tracers plus ricochets. The ribbon writes must stop at `MAX_RIBBONS` without throwing, and the heads at `MAX_BILLBOARDS`. Task 2 builds a frame over full counts.
- **Remote shots from an older shot word** that says `tracer: false` (a shooter whose gun had `tracer_every` 5): with matched builds this cannot happen, but `fireRemote` must still colour by the seat's team when it is a tracer and draw nothing when not. Task 1 has a test.
- **Ricochet determinism:** two builds of the same frame's events must draw the same ricochets, since a ricochet's draw comes from a hash of its impact, not `Math.random`. Task 3 has a test.

- Modify: `src/render/shaders/effects.wgsl`:
  - The `Ribbon` struct gains `style: f32, coreShare: f32, glowShare: f32, white: f32`.
  - **Style 0** is today's profile exactly.
  - **Style 1** (a tracer):
    - drawn width `clamp(width, MIN_RIBBON_PX·pixel, maxPx·pixel)`. `maxPx` is `tracer_glow_max_px`, carried in the `EffectsDraw` uniform, where `pad0: u32` becomes `glowMaxPx: f32` (written as a float by `Effects.draw`);
    - across, `core·exp(-u²/σc²)` mixed toward white by `white`, plus `glowShare·exp(-u²/σg²)·rgb`;
    - σc taken from coreShare of the drawn width;
    - light kept as `width / drawn` as today.
- Modify: `src/combat/ballistics.ts`: `tracerSegments(lengthOf: (speed: number) => number)` (a number still accepted).
- Modify: `src/app/effects-frame.ts`:
  - **The streak:** each tracer's is `clamp(speed × streakS, minM, maxM)`, capped by flown.
  - **Lit in:** alphas multiplied by `ignite(f) = min(1, f / igniteM)` at the tail's and the head's flown distances.
  - **The head:** a billboard at the head once flown ≥ `headFromM`. Emissive, its colour the core's radiance × `tracer_head_gain` / `particle_glow` (the light pass multiplies emissive billboards by it).
  - **The battle's tracers:** the same look, their streak `clamp(speed × streakS, minM, battleTrailM)`, their heads too.
  - **By day:** `build(..., daylight = 0)` multiplies every tracer's radiance by `1 + dayBoost × daylight`.
  - `counts.heads` added.
- Modify: `src/app/main.ts`: pass `daylight = smoothstep(-4, 20, sunElevationDeg)` from the sky state the frame uses into `effects.build`.
- Test:

  - `tests/combat/ballistics.test.ts` (`tracerSegments` with a function);
