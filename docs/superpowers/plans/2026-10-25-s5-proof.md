# Showcase, Milestone 5: Proof Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The portfolio card is ready to paste, with its square thumbnail and its note on how, and a capture shows it among the user's projects. The showcase's review captures are collected. The whole suite, the production load and Safari are run and recorded. Nothing is published, and the user's site is not touched.

**Architecture:**
- **The stills tool** also makes the card's square thumbnail: the still's middle 1440 × 1440, scaled to 1080, as WebP. The spec's §2 has this third form, deferred from S2.
- **`showcase/portfolio/`** holds:
  - `card.html`: the card in the site's `details-container color-container` markup;
  - `trenchline.webp`;
  - `README.md`.
- **A review capture** opens `~/repos/portfolio-website/index.html` read-only through `file://`, puts the card first among its projects in the browser's copy of the page, and photographs it.

**Tech Stack:** HTML, Playwright (Chrome), Vitest, cwebp, ffmpeg.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-trenchline-web-showcase-design.md (done-table rows 5 and 7; §5; the review captures; build order item 5).

## Global Constraints

- **The user's site is read, never written.** No file under `~/repos/portfolio-website` changes. It is not under version control, so a change there could not be undone by git. Pasting the card is the user's go-ahead, asked at the end.
- **Nothing is published:** no push, no merge, Pages not turned on.
- **The card's links:**
  - https://github.com/LordJord78/Voxel-Trench-Warfare;
  - https://lordjord78.github.io/Voxel-Trench-Warfare/ (live once published).
- **Every earlier test passes;** the production prompt within 4 s; Safari's report run and recorded (not gated).
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work stays on `web/world`.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **The thumbnail is WebP** (≤ 120 KB), not the site's PNG: its `<img>` takes either, and a PNG of a rendered dusk at 1080² is about ten times the size.
2. **The card goes first among the projects,** above the three placeholders, which the user keeps or replaces; the note says so.
3. **The capture is skipped, with a reason, where the user's site is absent,** as on another machine.

## Review Focus

- **The user's site untouched:** its files' sizes and times the same before and after the capture. Pinned in Task 2.
- **The card in the site's own style:** its classes, the image's square, the buttons' links. Pinned in Tasks 1 and 2.
