# Light, Milestone 3: The Camera's Response Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:**
- Give the image a camera's response to the light: bloom, exposure that adapts to what the frame shows, and film grain.
- Keep the approved dusk and night look, and the whole day under 2% a game minute.

**Architecture:**
- **Bloom** is a six-level dual-filter pyramid over the sharpened HDR image (13-tap down, tent up), mixed into it in the present pass with no threshold, conserving energy.
- **Exposure** keeps milestone 2's reckoning from the light (exposure.wgsl) as its base. A 128-bin log-luminance histogram of the frame, taken under that base, measures how far the view sits from the norm. Inside a dead zone the base stands, so the approved views keep their exposures. Beyond it the exposure adapts toward the zone's edge, 3 stops a second brighter and 1 darker. The hour's bias stays on top.
- **Grain** is blue-noise luminance grain in the present pass, keyed to the clock, animated each frame.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WGSL compute and render passes, Vitest, Playwright GPU cases.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-trenchline-web-light-design.md (§4 The camera's response; done-table rows 1, 4, 6).

## Global Constraints

- **Bloom:** weight 0.04 (`render.bloom_weight`), no threshold, six levels; the mix conserves the image's energy within 1%.
- **Histogram:** 128 bins of log2 luminance; the darkest 50% and the brightest 2% of pixels are ignored; NaN and infinite pixels are never counted.
- **Adaptation:** 3 stops a second toward brighter, 1 toward darker, in real time (frame dt, capped at 0.1 s); a history reset (teleport, resize, a gap) snaps to the target.
- **The approved look:** every standard view at its own hour keeps the exposure milestone 2 gives it, within 0.1 stop (dusk 1.0 and night 3.2 in the night view).
- **Row 4:** a whole day at ten game minutes a second changes the frame by under 2% a game minute, and never jumps (each frame within 2% of the last).
- **Perf:** every standard view ≤ 14 ms at dusk, night, noon and dawn (dynamic resolution off, 1.5 MP). Bloom, histogram and adaptation together ≤ 0.6 ms in the overview.

## Review Focus

- **The approved views.** A pure histogram exposure would bring every view to one brightness: the bay brighter, the overview darker than approved. The dead zone must hold all six views at their own hour. Pinned in Task 2 (the exposures of the standard views against milestone 2's).
- **A NaN or infinite pixel.** It must not reach the histogram, and the exposure must stay finite. Pinned in Task 2 (a poisoned image).
- **Dynamic resolution.** The histogram covers the rendered part of the texture (`res`), not its allocation. Pinned in Task 2 (a scaled frame).
- **Time-lapse and pause.** Adaptation runs in real seconds; the day cycle and a paused game must not jump. Pinned in Task 2 (row 4's cycle, and convergence by dt).
- **The sun through bloom.** The sun's disc (radiance up to 30,000) must spread into a halo, not ring or block, and leave no NaN. Pinned in Task 1 (a point source's energy and shape).

  - `the sun's disc spreads into a halo`: the same point; the bloomed values fall monotonically outward along a row from it, none negative or NaN, and 32 pixels out still above the noise's mean by a tenth of the halo's peak.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: FAIL (no bloom pass).

The present pass mixes `(1 − w)·image + w·bloom/6`, where bloom is the up chain's top read bilinearly; six levels each conserving energy make the average conserve it.

### Task 2: Exposure that adapts to the frame

**Files:**
- Create: `src/render/shaders/histogram.wgsl` (entries `count` and `adapt`), `tests/support/exposure-ref.ts`, `tests/render/exposure-ref.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/render/shaders/exposure.wgsl` (the base only: it writes the reckoned exposure without the bias to the adaptation state), `src/render/renderer.ts` (the histogram and adaptation passes after `cas`; `frame.dt`; reset snaps), `src/render/frame-uniforms.ts` (`dt`, `exposureTarget`, `exposureZone`), `src/render/sky-state.ts` (`frameSky` carries them), `src/app/main.ts` (dt into the renderer), `data/tunables.toml` (`sky.exposure_target`, `sky.exposure_zone`, `sky.adapt_up`, `sky.adapt_down`), `tests/e2e/day.spec.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's present uniform (`exposure` at index 6, unchanged in meaning: the exposure the frame is presented at).
- Produces: an `exposure` storage buffer (`base`, `correction` in stops, `target` in stops, the metered mean), readable by `Renderer.exposureState()` for tests; `Renderer.exposure()` keeps returning the presented exposure.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.**
  - Vitest, `exposure-ref.test.ts`, against `tests/support/exposure-ref.ts` (the reference the GPU is checked by): `meteredMean(histogram)` ignores the darkest 50% and brightest 2% (a histogram with all its mass in two bins gives the upper bin's value); `zoneCorrection(target, metered, zone)` is 0 within the zone and the excess beyond it; `adapt(c, want, dt, up, down)` moves at most `up·dt` brighter and `down·dt` darker, and lands on `want` when nearer.
  - GPU (`response.spec.ts`): `the histogram counts the frame as the CPU does` (a known image; exact bin counts; NaN and infinite pixels uncounted; with dynamic resolution at 0.6, only the rendered part counted); `the exposure converges at its rates` (a scene 4 stops brighter than the zone: within 0.05 stop of the zone's edge after (4 − zone) seconds of 60 Hz frames, never faster; the reverse at 3 stops a second; a history reset lands on it at once).
  - e2e (`day.spec.ts`): `every standard view keeps its approved exposure` (each view at its own hour: the presented exposure within 0.1 stop of the reckoned one); `the lantern-lit dugout is brought down at night and in twilight` (at 21:00, and at 18:30 where the reckoned exposure peaks at 8.4 after milestone 2's fix, at least a stop under the reckoned exposure); the day cycle unchanged (under 2% a game minute, no jump, the outdoor exposure's peak under 2.75× the night's).
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
  - `count`: per pixel of the rendered region (`res`), the log2 luminance of `sharp` × the base exposure, binned over [−12, +4] stops in 128 bins (workgroup histograms in shared memory, merged by atomics); non-finite or non-positive values skipped.
  - `adapt` (one workgroup of 128): the metered mean; the zone's correction toward `exposureTarget` (stops, the norm the approved views sit round) beyond `exposureZone`; the rate-limited step by `dt`; a reset snaps. The presented exposure is bias × base × 2^correction, into the present uniform.
  - Set `exposure_target` and `exposure_zone` from the six standard views at their hours: the target the middle of their metered means, the zone half their spread plus 0.25 stop. Ledger the values.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** everything above and the perf spec. Expected: PASS; histogram and adaptation ≤ 0.25 ms in the overview.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** "Meter the frame, and adapt the exposure where the view leaves the norm".
