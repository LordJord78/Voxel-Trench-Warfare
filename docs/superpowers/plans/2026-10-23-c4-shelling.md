# Combat, Milestone 4: Shelling Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Shells land. A shell whistles in, flashes and lights what can see it, throws soil and debris, shakes the view, leaves smoke in the haze and a crater at 10 cm whose rim slumps into the trench and floods below the table. The crater is cut off the main thread and is in the world within half a second.

**Architecture:**
- **World side** (`src/world`, pure):
  - a region of bricks is copied out of the map, sector-aligned and full height;
  - a job runs generation's `detonate` on the copy and returns the bricks it changed, or asks for a wider region.
- **Sim side** (`src/sim`, `src/combat`, deterministic):
  - the session's destruction queue posts jobs to a runner (in-process in tests, a worker in play), holds marks made inside a region that is out, and writes the changed bricks back a budget a tick;
  - the front's artillery announces each shell `whistle_s` before it lands;
  - a landing throws soil and debris, shakes the view and suppresses.
- **App side:** a destruction worker runs the same job.
- **Render side:**
  - the blast's light is a transient light, shadowed at the lighting resolution, outside the denoiser;
  - smoke is spheres of extra haze in the froxels;
  - the key's height map is traced outright once a large crater's bricks are up.
- **Sound:** the whistle and the blast are synthesised voices.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WGSL, WebGPU, Web Workers, WebAudio, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-trenchline-web-combat-design.md:
- build order item 4;
- done-table row 3, and row 4's shell effects;
- §3 ("Cost and where it runs", "Ruling: a destruction worker", "Shells", "Incoming fire", "On the GPU");
- §4 ("Light from effects", "Smoke and dust in the haze");
- §5's whistle and blast.

Plus milestone 3's rulings and fixes (.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-22-c3-effects/progress.md): transient lights outside the denoiser; particles lit near faces by the face's light and seen through the haze; the flash timed in the world's seconds.

**Reference:** the reference has no runtime shelling. `world/destruction.ts`'s `detonate` is the port of its `detonate`, used by generation's direct hits (`world/generation/events.ts`). `[destruction]` holds its options.

## Global Constraints

- **Architecture rules** (tests/architecture.test.ts): `core`, `world`, `physics`, `sim` and `combat` import nothing from `render`, `ui`, `app` or `audio`; use no DOM, WebGPU, WebAudio, `Worker` or timing globals; and use only the exactly rounded `Math` functions, everything else from `core/dmath`.
- **Every magnitude comes from the tunables.** The spec's numbers:
  - shell radius 2.2–3.4 m, drawn from the stream `combat.shells`;
  - a whistle about 1.5 s before impact;
  - write-back under 4 ms a frame;
  - a blast light that flares and dies over 0.3 s;
  - smoke that rises, spreads and fades over about 15 s;
  - the key map traced outright after an edit of more than 64 bricks;
  - no nearer than 15 m unless the intensity is heavy;
  - in the world within 0.5 s; no frame's main-thread work over 20 ms while shells land.
- **Determinism.** Tests run the job in-process, in step with the ticks: the same seed and inputs give the same voxels. In play a crater lands whenever the worker finishes.
- **The approved look holds.** With no shell landed, nothing changes a standard view. The GPU frame stays ≤ 14 ms at 1.5 MP in Chrome for the standard views.
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work stays on `web/world`; nothing is pushed.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **Regions are whole sectors across and the world's full height.**
   - The box is the blast's reach (`radius × max(loosen, 1) + 2`) plus 48 voxels: the collapse's pad of 24 and one widening of 24. It is widened outward to whole sectors (32 voxels) in x and z, and spans y = 0 to the top, because the collapse follows columns down to the first solid and up through what rests on them.
   - The job's map is a `BrickMap` of the region's size, so its dims are multiples of 32.
   - **Wider.** If the job's worked box reaches a side of its map that is not the world's edge, its collapse may have been cut short there. It returns `{ wider }`: the box grown 64 voxels each way. The queue copies that and posts again.
   - **Cost if wrong:** a crater spends one more copy and job.
2. **The write-back budget is a brick count, `bricks_per_tick` (512), not a clock.** The simulation may not read time. 512 bricks is about 1 ms (Task 2 measures it), far under the spec's 4 ms. An e2e measures the frame's main-thread time while shells land.
   - **Cost if wrong:** a slower device spends more than 4 ms on a crater's tick.
3. **Strictly in order.** A shell whose region overlaps one that is out waits, and so does every shell behind it.
   - **Cost if wrong:** a far shell behind a near pair lands a few hundred milliseconds late.
4. **A shell is announced `whistle_s` before it lands.** Its flight before that is not simulated. Its impact is the top of the ground under its (x, z): the first voxel that is not air, water included, down its column.
   - **Cost if wrong:** a shell through an overhang lands on top of it.
5. **The blast's light is a transient light, shadowed, outside the denoiser.** This reverses §4's "through the light buffer, so ReSTIR picks them", as milestone 3 did for the shot's light: a 0.3 s light through a 32-frame history would show late, dim and lingering.
   - **Instead:**
     - the lighting pass traces one shadow ray per lighting pixel to each live blast (at most 4) and writes their light to a texture of its own, with no history;
     - shade adds it, upsampled as the denoised light is;
     - the haze takes it in `integrate` (no history), unshadowed, as it takes the point lights;
     - particles take it whole, as they take the shot's;
     - the rifle takes it through its probe's visibility.
   - **Cost if wrong:** its shadows are as coarse as the lighting grid, and they step a little as the lighting's representative pixel moves.
6. **Smoke is analytic.** Each live sphere adds optical depth along a froxel column's ray (the chord through it times its density) in `integrate`, and is lit by the haze's own glow. The 16 nearest the eye are drawn. With the froxels off, and past 120 m, there is no smoke.
   - **Cost if wrong:** a seventeenth puff is not drawn.
7. **Blast lights and smoke are built on the render side**, from the drained blast events, aged by the world's time as the flash is. Soil and debris are sim-side particles, as milestone 3's are. Debris is drawn as square billboards: the billboard's last float is a kind (0 lit disc, 1 emissive disc, 2 lit square).
8. **A blast suppresses** within `blast_radius_m`, and shakes the view within `shake_radius_m`. The spec is silent; a shell 10 m off that neither pins nor shakes would read as wrong.
9. **The key map's outright trace.** It happens once, when a crater of more than 64 changed bricks has been written back and the upload queue is empty again. Uploads already re-trace a sixteenth a frame as before.

## Review Focus

- **The ground goes from under the player.** A crater cut where the player stands leaves them falling into it, or standing on what slumped. They are never inside solid, and never held in the air. Pinned in Task 4.
- **The worker fails or cannot start.** Shells still land, in-process, with a console warning, and the game does not stop. Pinned in Task 3.
- **A shell at the world's edge, or with the region clamped there.** It lands without error, and its crater matches `detonate` in place. Pinned in Task 1.
- **Heavy fire's overlapping shells.** Ten shells on one spot in ten seconds all land, in order, each as `detonate` in place would cut it after the last. Pinned in Task 2.
- **Paused while a region is out.** Nothing is written while no tick runs, and the crater lands after play resumes. Pinned in Task 2.
