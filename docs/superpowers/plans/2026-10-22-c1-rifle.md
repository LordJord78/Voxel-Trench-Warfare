# Combat, Milestone 1: The Rifle and Its Rounds Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The player fires the reference's 5.45 rifle from the eye at 650 rounds a minute. Each round flies with the reference's drag and drop through the 10 cm world, stops at the first solid voxel, and marks it.

**Architecture:**
- **Raycast.** A CPU raycast through the brick map (`world/raycast.ts`) finds hits. It steps across an empty brick without reading its voxels (Task 1's ruling).
- **Ballistics.** The reference's ballistics, ported to a struct-of-arrays pool (`combat/ballistics.ts`), integrate the rounds at 240 Hz.
- **The weapon.** `combat/weapon.ts` holds the rifle: its timer, magazine, reload and dispersion.
- **The session.** It fires from the eye each tick the trigger asks, steps the rounds, and lets each impact mark its voxel (`destruction.chip`). The world's existing dirty-brick path carries the marks to the GPU.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-trenchline-web-combat-design.md, build order item 1; done-table row 1; §1 "The rifle and its rounds" with its rulings.

**Reference:**
- reference/python/trenchline/combat/weapons.py and ballistics.py;
- reference/python/trenchline/world/destruction.py (`chip`) and raycast.py;
- reference/python/tests/test_ballistics.py and test_destruction.py's chip cases.

## Global Constraints

- **The rifle:** 650 rpm, a 30-round magazine, a 2.4 s reload. The reference's flight table (at 0.125 s: 103 m, 773 m/s, 7.6 cm of drop) and the flat-fire closed form (100 m in 0.121 s, at 776 m/s) hold, each within 2% (Task 2's ruling).
- **Marks:** a hit marks its voxel at ≥ 260 J × the material's hardness (`[destruction] chip_energy_j`). Soil becomes spoil, timber becomes rubble, a branch becomes air (`spoilOf`).
- **Aim:** the origin is the eye. The aim is the view's yaw and pitch plus a dispersion drawn at the muzzle, from the named stream `combat.dispersion`. The dispersion is a uniform disc of 3.2 MOA × the stance's `sway_scale`. Recoil and flinch come in milestone 2.
- **Integrator:**
  - explicit quadratic drag, then gravity, then semi-implicit Euler, at 4 substeps of the 60 Hz tick, in float64;
  - a pool of 256, struct-of-arrays, live rounds a prefix, retirement by swap-with-last;
  - every value from `[ballistics]` and `[weapon.assault_rifle]`, none in code.
- **What stops a round:** a solid voxel (`isSolid`). Water, wire, cloth and air let it through.
- **Retirement:** a round leaving the world's box is retired, and so is one past 400 m or 4 s. The far field is not hit.
- **Rulings from the spec:**
  - the fire timer carries its remainder, so a held trigger fires 5 and 6 ticks apart in turn;
  - `automatic = false` fires once per press;
  - a full pool refuses the shot and the magazine is unchanged.
- **Determinism:** `src/combat` uses `core/dmath` for every transcendental function and imports nothing from render, ui or app. The architecture test covers it.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Rulings made in planning

- **An impact's distance is the path length to the hit point.** The reference's was the substep's end, up to 3.7 m long. The speed at impact is still the substep's start speed, as in the reference.
  - Cost if wrong: an impact's energy and distance differ from the reference's by under one substep's drag.
- **No shot leaves in the periscope stance.** The reference fired from the 2.2 m mirror head. A man holding a periscope is not holding his rifle.
  - Cost if wrong: none (milestone 3 hides the rifle there).
- **The fire timer is capped at one interval while the trigger rests.** A fresh pull fires at once, without a catch-up burst.
- **The trigger reaches the session as intent** (`MoveIntent.fire`, `MoveIntent.reload`).
  - In play: the left mouse button with the pointer locked, and R.
  - In headless tests: a hook (`trigger(held)`), since headless Chrome refuses pointer lock.
  - Dynamic resolution's developer key moves from R to Y.

## Review Focus

- **Firing while paused, flying or before play:** no round leaves. Pinned in Task 6.
- **The eye inside a solid voxel** (pressed to a parapet, prone in the mud): the round hits at once and marks the voxel it starts in. Pinned in Task 2.
- **Rounds fired straight up or over the map's edge:** retired with no error, and the pool never leaks. Pinned in Task 2.
- **A long frame with a full pool:** five catch-up ticks with 256 rounds in flight take at most 4 ms on the main thread. Pinned in Task 5.
- **Reloading at the edges:**
  - R while full or already reloading is refused, and no round is lost;
  - the trigger held through a reload fires again when it ends (automatic), while a semi-automatic rifle needs a new press.
  - Pinned in Task 3.

### Task 1: The raycast

**Files:**
- Create: `src/world/raycast.ts`
- Test: `tests/world/raycast.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  ```ts
  export interface RayHit {
    /** Voxels along the unit direction to where the ray entered the hit cell; 0 if it started inside one. */
    t: number;
    cell: [number, number, number];
    /** The face it came in through (0–5: −x, +x, −y, +y, −z, +z), −1 if it started inside. */
    face: number;
    /** The hit voxel's palette id. */
    material: number;
  }
  export function raycast(map: BrickMap, origin: readonly [number, number, number], dir: readonly [number, number, number], maxT: number): RayHit | null;
  ```
  The origin and `maxT` are in voxels, and `dir` is a unit vector. The ray is clipped to the world. Ties between axes go to the lowest, as in `tests/support/trace-ref.ts`.

  ```ts
  export interface ProjectileSpec { name: string; muzzleVelocity: number; massKg: number; /** 0.5 · Cd · A / m */ dragFactor: number }
  export function projectileSpec(tun: Tunables, weapon: string): ProjectileSpec;
  export interface BallisticsTunables { gravity: number; airDensity: number; substeps: number; capacity: number; maxRangeM: number; maxFlightS: number }

    normal: [number, number, number];   // (0,0,0) if it started inside
    cell: [number, number, number];     // voxels
    material: number; speed: number; distance: number; energyJ: number; owner: number; spec: string;

    position(slot: number): [number, number, number];
    velocity(slot: number): [number, number, number];
    tracerSegments(lengthM: number): { head: Float32Array; tail: Float32Array };
  }
  ```

    - `'a round leaving over the map's edge is retired'`: fired along +x from x 12.7 m; the pool is empty within 10 ticks, with no impact.
    - `'a round fired from inside a solid voxel hits it at once'`:
      - origin inside the concrete;
      - one impact at its cell with normal (0, 0, 0), distance 0;
      - the pool empty after the first step.
    - `'the pool is a ceiling and says so'`: capacity 4, 7 fires; the last 3 return −1, `dropped` is 3 and `count` is 4.
    - `'retiring a round does not disturb the others'`: after a swap-remove, the surviving slow round keeps x = 9.0.
    - `'tracer segments trail behind the head'`:
      - only tracer rounds appear;
      - `tail.z < head.z`;
      - `|head − tail|` ≤ the length;
      - an empty pool gives empty arrays.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `ProjectileSpec`, `projectileSpec` (Task 2).
- Produces:
  ```ts
  export interface WeaponSpec { key: string; name: string; rpm: number; magazine: number; dispersionMoa: number; reloadS: number; automatic: boolean; tracerEvery: number; projectile: ProjectileSpec }
  export function weaponSpec(tun: Tunables, key: string): WeaponSpec;
  export class Weapon {
    constructor(spec: WeaponSpec);
    readonly spec: WeaponSpec;
    rounds: number; shotsFired: number;
  ```

  - **`'the trigger held through a reload fires when it ends; semi-automatic needs a new press'`.**

    - The maximum ≤ 1.45 × (3.2/60).
    - The median ≈ 0.707 × (3.2/60), within 15%.
  - **`'the same seed puts the same burst on the same aims'`;** a different seed differs.
  - **`'every fifth shot is a tracer'`.**
