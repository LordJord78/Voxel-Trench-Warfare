# Battle, Milestone 4: Fires and Their Smoke Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Shells that strike timber, cloth, duckboards or a hull start fires, and at night the hamlet's ruins burn. A fire flickers and casts shadows, throws flames and embers, smokes into the haze leaning downwind, chars what it burned, and crackles close by.

**Architecture:**
- **Simulation (`src/battle/fires.ts`, pure):** a fire is a pure function of its start and the time:
  - its growth, burn and dying down;
  - its flames and embers, as closed-form billboards;
  - its plume, as six haze puffs leaning with the wind;
  - its crackle's pops.
- **The director** keeps the fires:
  - the session's strikes start them by chance, fed in with the inputs;
  - the hamlet's are placed by the plan and lit at nightfall;
  - at most 12 burn.
- **Render side:**
  - each fire is a light of a new kind, `'fire'`, flickering, in the light buffer, which already shadows every light;
  - its flames and embers are billboards;
  - its plume goes into the haze, which takes 64 puffs, gathered once a froxel column;
  - char is the world's palette, burnt shades put in place once the strike's crater has settled.
- **Sound:** the crackle's pops, voiced within 30 m.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WGSL, WebGPU, WebAudio, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

## Global Constraints

- **Architecture rules** hold for `src/battle/`: no imports from `render`, `ui`, `app` or `audio`, and only the exactly rounded `Math` functions.
- **The spec's values:**
  - Fires start with a chance: a third for timber, cloth and duckboards, a fifth for a hull.
  - At night the hamlet's ruins burn: 2–4 fires placed in its buildings by the plan.
  - At most 12 fires burn at once; past that the oldest goes out.
  - A fire grows over 20 s, burns 2–6 minutes and dies down over a minute.
  - Its light: the lanterns' flicker, stronger and faster; radius 12–25 m by its size; warm orange.
  - A plume is a chain of six haze puffs.
  - The haze takes the 64 puffs nearest the camera; the low preset 32.
  - What a fire burned is charred and stays so.
  - Its crackle is heard within 30 m.
  - A fire does not spread.
- **Every magnitude comes from the tunables,** in a new `[battle.fires]` table and new `[audio]` keys.
- **Determinism.** The same seed, inputs (strikes among them) and steps give the same fires. Seeking replays the hamlet's fires but not the session's strikes, which it never had.
- **The approved look holds.** At rest there are no fires. The views held at rest draw as approved, including the haze with no puffs.
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work stays on `web/world`, and nothing is pushed.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **Only the session's shells start fires near the player.**
   - The spec's "the director's near shells" do not exist. B2's far shells land on the far field's height field, which has no timber.
   - A strike is the session's `blasts` event: where it struck, the material of the column's top voxel there, and its crater's id (added to the event).
2. **The hamlet's fires:**
   - 2–4 of its buildings, picked by the plan's stream `battle.fires.hamlet`.
   - Each fire stands on its building's rubble floor, its middle 1/3 of the way into the floor from the wall with a timber beam, if there is one.
   - They are lit at nightfall (the battle's dark above `dark_above`) and burn while it is dark. They are not held to the 2–6 minutes, because the spec has the ruins burning all night. They die down over a minute once it lifts.
   - They are outside the cap's eviction: the cap of 12 drops the oldest strike fire first.
3. **A fire's life:**
   - It grows (smoothstep over 20 s), burns 2–6 minutes (drawn at its start) and dies down over 60 s.
   - Its size (0.5–1.0, drawn) sets the light's radius, 12–25 m.
   - Its strength (0–1 through its life) scales the light, the flames, the embers and the plume.
4. **The light:**
   - Kind `'fire'`, 0.3 m over its fuel (the shadow ray stops 1.5 voxels short of a light).
   - Colour `[1.0, 0.55, 0.2]`, intensity 40 × strength.
   - It flickers as a lantern does but faster and stronger: the same three beats at 3× their rates, by 0.25 rather than 0.08.
   - Fire lights are appended in the light step as the battle's flares are, not passed through `lightsAt`.
5. **Flames and embers** are closed-form billboards from the fire, the time and its id, as the far shells' columns are:
   - 10 flames, each rising 1.5 m over its 0.8 s cycle (phase by its index), shrinking and fading, emissive orange-yellow;
   - 8 embers, each a 4 s cycle, rising 2 m/s and carried by the wind since it left, small and emissive.

   `MAX_BILLBOARDS` gains a battle allowance of 512 (B2's review: the battle's billboards had the 64 spare slots meant for test quads).
6. **The plume** is six puffs, as a far shell's column is (puff k rises to (k+1)/6 of 12 m, carried by the wind in that share, growing 1.5→4 m), at density 0.15 × strength.
   - The haze takes 64: the plumes' and the blasts' puffs, the nearest the eye. The low preset takes 32.
   - Because 64 puffs per slice per column would quadruple the haze's smoke cost, `integrate` first gathers once a column the puffs its ray passes through (at most 16). It then adds each slice's depth from those in closed form.
7. **Char:**
   - New palette shades are appended after `RIFLE_GRIP`: `CHAR_TIMBER`, `CHAR_CLOTH`, `CHAR_HULL`, the duckboards taking `CHAR_TIMBER`. Their kinds stay the material's, so only their colour changes.
   - Once a fire is at full burn, everything flammable within 1.5 m of it (timber, cloth, duckboard and hull voxels) is set to its burnt shade.
   - Char is applied in the app (`charAround(map, centre, radiusM)` in `src/world`) only once no crater region about it is out (`Destruction.overlapsOut`), retried each frame.
   - It stays: a fire's char is never undone.
8. **The crackle:**
   - Each burning fire pops at a Poisson rate of 8 × strength a second, drawn in the director's fire stream.
   - Each pop within 30 m is a short voice (a highpassed burst and a click), quieter with distance.
   - Pops beyond 30 m are not voiced. The cap holds the rest.

## Review Focus

- **A strike in the bay.** A shell on the parapet's timber or the duckboards at dusk starts a fire there a third of the time. The fire burns in what is left of the bay after the crater, chars the timber about it once the crater has settled, and is seen and heard from the firing step. Pinned in Tasks 2, 5 and 7.
- **Twelve.** Under a heavy front, when many shells strike timber, the thirteenth strike fire puts out the oldest strike fire, never a hamlet fire, and the frame holds its budget. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 7.
- **Rest unchanged.** At rest no fire burns, no puff is added, nothing is charred, and the approved views draw as before. Pinned in Tasks 3 and 4.
- **Dawn.** The hamlet's fires die down as the dark lifts, over a minute, and are not relit until nightfall. Pinned in Task 2.
- **The haze's cost.** With 64 puffs, the froxels' smoke stays within the budget at the overview at night. Pinned in Tasks 3 and 7.
