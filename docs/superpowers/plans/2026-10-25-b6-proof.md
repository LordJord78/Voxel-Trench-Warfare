## Global Constraints

- **The budget is sub-project 3's:** 14 ms GPU at the 95th percentile at 1.5 MP (the high preset, dynamic resolution off), 8 ms main thread at the 99th, no frame over 20 ms; at every standard view, at dusk and at night.
- **The battle at its peak:** the heavy intensity, the maximum flares (16 alight at most) and fires (12), and a barrage and tracer streams in progress — with sub-project 3's combat peak (a magazine on full auto, a shell a second 20–50 m off, the front heavy) on top.
- **Safari is measured under the same and reported**, not gated.
- **The load budget:** the production build shows its prompt within 4 s (`node tools/preview-gen.mjs --prod`).
- **Every earlier test passes**, the whole suite on mains power.
- **The approved look holds at rest;** no retune of haze_shadow / haze_lights.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **The peak's moment:** sought as perf.spec's heavy-night test seeks it — from 120 s into the heavy battle in steps of 5 s, the first moment with at least 15 flares burning and a barrage in progress (at night), or a barrage in progress (at dusk); 900 s at most, then the best found. Seeking replays the battle; the fires are struck after.
2. **The fires:** twelve timber strikes on the ground about the player's feet, 8–30 m off around a circle, fed to the battle (`advanceBattle`) and grown 25 s, so they burn at full strength in view, their lights, flames and plumes on screen.
3. **Peak combat under the battle:** `underFire(game, view, hours, seconds, { battle: true })` stands the player at the view, sets the battle's peak, then fires as before. Without `battle`, as before (the gate's own history stays comparable).
4. **The check flips to the battle:** its peak combat runs with `battle: true`, so Chrome's check and Safari's report measure both peaks; its views and the overview through the day stay at rest (the approved look's measures).
5. **Savings are chosen by measurement,** in this order of preference: what costs at rest and does nothing there (the reflect pass's fixed cost, the haze's smokeless gather), then what the battle adds that the eye cannot tell from less (flare maps at the overview's distance), then dynamic resolution's floor. Each cut is ledgered with its measured gain.
6. **The low preset's four flare maps** go to the nearest four burning flares; the rest light unshadowed (as the ninth and on do now).
