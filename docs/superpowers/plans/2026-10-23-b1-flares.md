# Battle, Milestone 1: Flares Fired and Drifting Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** At night both lines fire flares that climb, burst, drift on the wind, sink and gutter out, lighting and shadowing the field. They replace sub-project 2's six static flares while the lights run.

**Architecture:**
- **Simulation (`src/battle/`, deterministic and pure, like `combat`):**
  - a wind that is a pure function of time;
  - a flare's flight, a pure function of its launch, the time and the wind;
  - a director that schedules launches from posts on both front lines by the front's intensity and the dark, ticked at 60 Hz on the lights' clock. It can be sought to any moment by running from its start.
- **Render side:**
  - **At rest** (`holdLights()`, which every test has by default): the six planned flares, as now.
  - **Running:** the director's flares, which also drive the flares' shadow maps (now keyed by a flare's id, the eight nearest the player holding them) and the front's glow on the haze.
- **Sound:** a pop and a hiss at each launch.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WGSL, WebGPU, WebAudio, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

## Global Constraints

- **Architecture rules** (tests/architecture.test.ts): `src/battle/` joins `core`, `world`, `physics`, `sim` and `combat`. It imports nothing from `render`, `ui`, `app` or `audio`, uses no DOM, WebGPU, WebAudio or timing globals, and only the exactly rounded `Math` functions (sqrt, floor, ceil, round, trunc, abs, sign, min, max, imul, fround, clz32, no `**`); everything else comes from `core/dmath`.
- **Every magnitude comes from the tunables,** in a new `[battle]` table:
  - the flares': burst 80–120 m, elevation 70–85°, sink 0.3 m/s, burn 40 s, ignite 0.5 s, fade 5 s, radius 65 m, intensity 260, about six burning at `light`, ×2.5 at `heavy`, one a minute `off`, only while the sky's `flares` key is above 0.1;
  - the wind's: 1–5 m/s, veering a few degrees a minute about a prevailing direction from the enemy's side.
- **Determinism.** The same seed, inputs and clock steps give the same flares. Seeking to t gives what stepping to t gives.
- **The approved look holds.** At rest the six planned flares hang as now, so every test that holds the lights draws as before.
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work stays on `web/world`, and nothing is pushed.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **A flare's climb has no drag.** It is fired at the speed that brings it to its burst height at its elevation, `v0 = sqrt(2 g h) / sin(elevation)`. With drag, the apogee would come out of an integration rather than the spec's 80–120 m. **Cost if wrong:** a slightly straighter arc.
2. **The wind is a pure function of time:**
   - its bearing is the prevailing one plus two slow sines (periods 60–180 s, together at most `veer_deg`);
   - its speed is a mean plus two gust sines, kept within 1–5 m/s;
   - a drift is its integral by the midpoint rule in quarter-seconds, so a flare's place at any moment needs no stepping.
3. **Posts** are each front line's bays at their parapet crest (the front's own `planSource` pattern), on both lines:
   - a quarter of launches come from the posts within 60 m (along the line) of the player;
   - a flare is fired toward no man's land, within ±20° of across;
   - the friendly line and the enemy's fire equally.
4. **The schedule** is Poisson per tick at a total rate of the mean count over a flare's life: about 6 ÷ 45 s at `light`, ×2.5 at `heavy`, 1 ÷ 60 s at `off`. There are no launches while the sky's `flares` key is ≤ 0.1. A flare already in the air burns out whatever the hour.
5. **Rest and running.**
   - `holdLights()` (rest) draws the six planned flares at full burn, as now.
   - Running draws the director's.
   - `holdLights(t)` draws the director sought to t with the inputs of the moment, and caches it until the hold or the inputs change.
6. **Shadow maps follow a flare's id,** not its index in the frame's lights. The eight burning flares nearest the eye hold them.
7. **The front's glow** is its keyed colour × the burning flares' glow summed over 6, at most 2. At rest that is 1, as now.

## Review Focus

- **Pausing.** In the pause menu the battle clock stops: no flare moves, launches or burns down until play resumes. Pinned in Task 4.
- **Through the hours.** In time-lapse from dusk into night, flares start once the sky's `flares` key passes 0.1. At dawn, none is fired after it falls, and a flare already alight burns out rather than vanishing. Pinned in Task 3.
- **A heavy night.** About 15 burn at once: eight hold maps, the rest light the field unshadowed, and the frame holds the budget at the overview at night. Pinned in Task 6.
- **Rest unchanged.** Every view with the lights held draws exactly as before (the screenshots' and perf's held tests). Pinned in Task 4.
- **The player's sector.** A quarter of flares come from near the player, so a visitor in any bay sees flares overhead within a minute at `light`. Pinned in Task 3.
