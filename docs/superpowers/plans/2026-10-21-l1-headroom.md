# Light, Milestone 1: Headroom Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Buy the GPU time sub-project 2 needs: every standard view ≤ 11 ms at dusk and at night, at 1.5 MP. Then fix sub-project 1's carried blemishes: the lantern's dark frame, far-field facets, and TAA and fog hardening.

**Architecture:**
- **Primary rays:** a 2.5D beam pre-pass. One beam per 8 × 8 tile walks the max-height pyramid along the tile's frustum, and every pixel ray in the tile starts where the frustum can first meet anything.
- **À-trous filter:** the passes read their tile once into workgroup memory.
- **Point-light pick:** scans the lights once instead of twice.
- **Small shader fixes** cover the rest.

**Tech Stack:** WGSL compute, TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright GPU cases.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-trenchline-web-light-design.md (§1 Budget first, §4 hardening, §3 the lantern's own voxel, §6 far field).

## Review Focus

- **Thin occluders and grazing tiles.** A post, wire picket or tree trunk that the beam's frustum touches must still be hit by every pixel ray that would hit it. The beam may only skip space the pyramid proves empty for the whole frustum. Pinned in Task 1 (bit-identical hits over 8,192 rays, including grazing tiles over a field of posts).
- **Cameras inside the volume, below the column tops** (bays, dugouts). The beam must cost nearly nothing there and start every ray at 0. Pinned in Task 1 (the dugout view's beam distances are all 0).
- **Tiles at the image edge, and resolution changes** (dynamic resolution). Partial tiles and a scaled image read the beam texture at the right tile. Pinned in Task 1 (scale.spec stays green; an edge-tile case).
- **À-trous halos at the image edge.** Clamped loads must give exactly the old result. Pinned in Task 2 (the filtered image equals the old pass's output within f16 rounding).

### Task 1: The beam pre-pass

**Files:**
- Create: `src/render/shaders/beam.wgsl`
- Modify: `src/render/shaders/primary.wgsl` (start at the tile's beam distance), `src/render/renderer.ts` (the beam pass and its texture), `tests/e2e/cases/traverse.ts` or `primary.ts` (cases)
- Test: `tests/e2e/primary.spec.ts` (extend), `tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts` (gate 11 ms during this milestone)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: the texture `beam` (r32float) with ceil(res / 8) texels: the distance, in voxels along each pixel's ray, before which no ray in the tile can hit anything. It is 0 where the camera is below the pyramid's tops in the tile's frustum.
- `FLAGS.BEAM_OFF` (16) turns it off, for the bit-identity test.

**The beam, for a tile:**
1. Take the frustum of the tile's corner rays, jitter included.
2. March the central ray's distance t in steps of one brick (8 voxels), growing the step with pyramid levels, as traverse.wgsl's pyramid skip does.
3. For each step [t0, t1], bound the frustum's xz extent by its corner points at t0 and t1, plus one brick.
4. Find that bound's highest column from the pyramid level whose cell covers it: at most four reads.
5. If the frustum's lowest y over the step is above that height, the step is empty: advance. Otherwise stop, and write t0 converted to each corner ray's own parameter: the minimum over the corners.
6. Rays that leave the volume (far field, sky) give the volume's exit, and the far-field march starts there as today.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - **Bit-identity.** A `primary` case renders a synthetic world with the beam on and with BEAM_OFF. The world is a field with 200 posts one voxel wide, a wire fence of single voxels, and a raised mound. Grazing cameras are at 0.3 m over the field, looking along it. Every pixel's gHit and gDepth are equal: 0 differences in 16,384 pixels across 4 poses.
  - **Distances are ≤ the hit.** On the same poses, every pixel's beam distance is ≤ its own hit distance; one-ulp ties are allowed.
  - **Inside a dugout:** all beam distances are 0.
  - **Perf.** `perf.spec.ts` asserts each view ≤ 11 ms at dusk and night. It fails on overview, nml and bay at night today.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx playwright test tests/e2e/primary.spec.ts tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts`. Expected: FAIL. The new cases fail because there is no beam; perf fails as above.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** beam.wgsl and the primary start.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** primary.spec and the scale and denoise specs. Expected: PASS. Then run the per-pass table (the perf spec prints it) and ledger the primary pass's change per view.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** "Start primary rays where their tile's frustum can first meet the world".

### Task 2: À-trous in workgroup memory

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/render/shaders/atrous.wgsl`
- Test: `tests/e2e/denoise.spec.ts` (existing, plus a new identity case)

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing test.** A `denoise` case runs one à-trous pass at each step, 1, 2 and 4, on a fixed noisy lighting image with plane and face edges, and saves the output. The test compares it with a copy of today's shader (`tests/e2e/shaders/atrous-reference.wgsl`, taken before the change). They must be equal within one f16 ulp per channel. It must be written and run before the change: it passes against itself, and it is the check that the rewrite changes nothing.
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement.**
  - 8 × 8 workgroups; each loads its 8 × 8 tile plus a halo of 2 × step, clamped at the image edge, into workgroup arrays:
    - colour as two `pack2x16float` words;
    - variance as (f32 variance, f32 length);
    - the hit as packed face and plane.
  - That is at most 24 × 24 × 20 B, about 11.5 KB, under WebGPU's 16 KB minimum.
  - The 25 taps and the 3 × 3 variance and neighbour-key reads then come from workgroup memory.
- [ ] **Step 3: Run** the denoise spec, including identity, and the perf spec. Ledger the à-trous change per view.
- [ ] **Step 4: Commit** "Filter the lighting from workgroup memory".
