# Battle, Milestone 3: Tracers Across the Sky Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** At night machine guns on both lines, beyond the stretch of front that fires at the player, fire bursts whose tracers stream over no man's land and the field. Some ricochet up, all fall, and the distant chatter is heard. The audio engine's voices are capped.

**Architecture:**
- **Simulation (`src/battle/`, pure and deterministic):**
  - A tracer's flight is closed-form in time: drag along its line of fire, gravity's drop under it, its strike on the field and its ricochet worked out when it is fired.
  - The director fires bursts by the front's intensity, in the dark, from guns away from the player.
- **Render side:** the battle's tracers are drawn as the effects pass's ribbons, as sub-project 3's are, the 400 nearest the eye.
- **Sound:** one chatter voice per burst, late by its distance. The engine keeps at most 64 voices, dropping the farthest first.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WGSL, WebGPU, WebAudio, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

## Rulings made in planning

1. **A tracer's flight is closed-form,** not stepped. With k the rifle round's drag factor × air density (the projectile the session flies):
   - along its line of fire it is at speed `v0 / (1 + k·v0·s)` and distance `ln(1 + k·v0·s) / k`, s seconds after it was fired;
   - under that, gravity drops it `g·s²/2`.

   This is a pure function of the shot and the time, so a seek needs no stepping. The drag does not act on the drop: it is the reference's projectile simplified, without strikes.
2. **Its end is worked out as it is fired:**
   - Its path is marched in 0.02 s steps against the field's undisturbed height (`fieldHeight`: the relief, with no trenches or craters) for up to `burn_s` (3 s, a tracer's burn).
   - It strikes where it first goes under the ground.
   - One in six strikes ricochets (drawn at firing). From the strike it flies again at 0.35 of its speed there, at a random bearing (all round) and 10–40° up, until the ground again or its burn ends.
3. **Guns:**
   - There is one at each firing bay of both front lines whose middle is more than `clear_m` (60 m) along the line from the player.
   - A burst is a gun picked uniformly: 5–20 rounds at 600 rpm, rounds 0, 5, 10 and 15 tracers. Its bearing is straight across ±35°, swept 10° over the burst, at 0.3–3° up, from 0.5 m over the crest.
   - A burst any of whose tracers' line of fire passes within `miss_m` (30 m) of the player across the ground, within its range, is not fired. Its draws are still made, so the stream stays the same.
4. **The tempo:** bursts come by Poisson at 5 a second at `light`, ×2.5 at `heavy`, none `off`, and only while the battle's dark is above `dark_above` (as the flares). At light that is ~2.5 tracers a burst × ~2 s aloft ≈ 25 in flight.
5. **Drawn** as sub-project 3's tracers are:
   - a bright head at the round, its trail `tracer_length_m` back along its velocity;
   - the effects' tracer colour × intensity, alpha from the tail's to 1.

   `MAX_RIBBONS` grows from 512 to 1024. At most `max_drawn` (400) are drawn, the nearest the eye first.
6. **The chatter:** one voice per burst at its gun, late by its distance (the distant law of B2, over [60, 300] m):
   - gain × 60 / d;
   - lowpass 2000 → 400 Hz;
   - its rounds at the burst's rpm, each a short low crack.
7. **The voice cap** is pure bookkeeping (`src/audio/cap.ts`). A voice holds a place from its play until its end (its delay plus its length).
   - At 64 a new voice farther than all held is not played.
   - Otherwise the farthest held is cut (its gain ramped out over 20 ms) and the new one plays.
   - `playVoice` returns the handle that cuts it.

## Review Focus

- **The player in a stream.** Wherever the player stands along the front, no battle tracer passes within 30 m when fired. A player walking under an old stream is not hit: tracers are seen and heard, not felt. Pinned in Task 2.
- **Through the hours.** At dawn, once the sun is up, no burst is fired. Tracers in flight finish their flight, and none is fired by day. Pinned in Task 2.
- **A heavy night's voices.** Bursts' chatter, the front's reports, the flares and the distant guns together hold the engine at 64 voices, the nearest kept, and the main thread within its budget. Pinned in Tasks 4 and 5.
- **Rest unchanged.** At rest no battle tracer is drawn or heard. Sub-project 3's own tracers still draw. Pinned in Task 3.
- **Many ribbons.** Under a heavy night with the combat peak's own tracers and flash stars, nothing is dropped from the session's ribbons for the battle's: the session's are drawn first. Pinned in Task 3.

### Task 1: A tracer's flight

**Files:**
- Create: `src/battle/tracers.ts`
- Modify: `data/tunables.toml`, `[battle.tracers]`:
  - `bursts_light = 5`, `heavy_scale = 2.5`;
  - `rounds = [5, 20]`, `rpm = 600`, `tracer_every = 5`;
  - `spread_deg = 35`, `sweep_deg = 10`, `elevation_deg = [0.3, 3]`, `muzzle_mps = 820`;
  - `burn_s = 3`, `ricochet_share = 0.1667`, `ricochet_speed = 0.35`, `ricochet_up_deg = [10, 40]`;
  - `clear_m = 60`, `miss_m = 30`, `max_drawn = 400`.
- Test: `tests/battle/tracers.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `interface TracerTunables`, `tracerTunables(tun: Tunables): TracerTunables`. Its drag `k` comes from `projectileSpec(tun, 'assault_rifle').dragFactor × ballisticsTunables(tun).airDensity`, and gravity from the ballistics' table;
  - `interface Leg { from: [n, n, n]; dir: [n, n, n]; v0: number; t0: number; t1: number }` (a straight-fired flight from t0, ending at t1);
  - `interface Shot { id: number; legs: Leg[] }` (one leg, or two if it ricocheted);
  - `planShot(id, origin, dir, t0, ground: (xM: number, zM: number) => number, rng: Rng, tt: TracerTunables): Shot`;
  - `interface TracerState { id: number; position: [n, n, n]; velocity: [n, n, n]; flown: number }`;
  - `tracerAt(shot: Shot, t: number, tt: TracerTunables): TracerState | null` (null before it is fired and after it ends).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `'a tracer flies its line of fire slowed by drag, and drops under gravity'`, on a flat ground far below. At 1 s:
    - its distance along the line is `ln(1 + k·v0) / k` (6 digits);
    - its height is the line's less `g/2`;
    - its speed along the line is `v0 / (1 + k·v0)`.
  - `'it ends where it first goes under the ground'`, on ground 2 m under the muzzle: the leg's end is within 0.02 s of where the path crosses it, and `tracerAt` after the end is null.
  - `'a ricochet climbs from where it struck, slower, at a random bearing'`. Over 600 shots fired down at the ground:
    - 1/6 ± 0.05 have a second leg;
    - each second leg starts at the first's end;
    - each climbs at 10–40°;
    - each is at 0.35 of the first's speed at its strike;
    - their bearings cover all four quarters.
  - `'it burns out after burn_s however it flies'`.
  - `'the same stream plans the same shot'`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement,** as rulings 1–2 set out. The ricochet's draws are made for every shot (chance, bearing, climb), whether or not it strikes, so the stream's order never depends on the ground.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run them to see them pass,** with `npm test` and `tsc`.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** ("Fly a tracer: drag, drop, strike and ricochet").
