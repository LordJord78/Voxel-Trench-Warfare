# Combat, Milestone 2: Recoil, View Kick and Suppression Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The rifle kicks and climbs as the reference's does. Its view kick moves only the picture. Rounds cracking past the player's head suppress them: pinned, pushed off the step, shaken and slowed. The front fires those rounds from the enemy line at the intensity the pause menu sets.

**Architecture:**
- **Feel.** Three deterministic modules ported from the reference:
  - `combat/recoil.ts` (aim) over an exact damped spring (`combat/spring.ts`);
  - `combat/viewkick.ts` (cosmetic);
  - `combat/suppression.ts`.
- **Cracks.** The rounds' pool reports near misses to a listener at the eye, segment by segment.
- **The front.** `combat/front.ts` fires the enemy line's rounds on a timer. It has no squads and no damage.
- **The session.** It runs them in the reference's tick order.
- **The camera.** The rig adds recoil, flinch, shake (with roll, new to the camera) and the FOV pulse to the view.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright (Chrome).

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-trenchline-web-combat-design.md, build order item 2; done-table row 2; §2; §3's "Incoming fire (the front)".

## Global Constraints

- **Every magnitude comes from the tunables.** `[recoil.defaults]` is overlaid by `[recoil.<weapon>]`, `[viewkick.defaults]` by `[viewkick.<weapon>]`, and `[suppression]` and `[front]` are read whole. None is written in code, and the reference's literal-scan tests are ported to hold it.
- **The spring:** exact closed-form steps of x″ = −ω²x − 2ζωx′, with ω = 5.83 / settle time. The constant 5.83 is the root of (1 + u)e^−u = 0.02.
- **Recoil:**
  - its kick is a displacement, peaking on the shot's tick;
  - its climb approaches its ceiling;
  - its drift starts after `drift_onset_shots`;
  - its scale is the stance's `sway_scale`;
  - its draws come from `combat.recoil`.
- **View kick:** six channels plus the FOV, displaced by each shot. It never moves a round. Its draws come from `combat.viewkick`.
- **Suppression:**
  - **cracks:** 0.34 × nearness², within 3.2 m of the head, per substep segment, from rounds that are not the player's;
  - **impacts:** 0.16 × (1 − d/4.5)², from rounds that are not the player's (the spec's ruling);
  - **the level:** holds 0.55 s, then decays 0.42 a second;
  - **flinch:** 1.35° × nearness, upward, with a sideways draw from `combat.suppression`;
  - **speed:** scaled to 1 − 0.6 × the level;
  - **pinned:** at ≥ 0.55;
  - **sway:** the cone is scaled by 1 + 3.2 × the level (the reference's dead multiplier, wired).
- **The front:** off, light or heavy (`[front]`), light by default for a visitor and off in the e2e harness unless a test asks. Its draws come from `front`.
- **Determinism:** `src/combat` keeps to `core/dmath` and the architecture rules.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Rulings made in planning

- **Suppression's shake rolls the camera.** The camera pose gains `roll` (degrees about the view's forward), so the reference's shake (pitch sin(1.7φ) · A, roll 0.7 sin(φ) · A) is drawn whole. Motion vectors follow it, since they come from the poses.
  - Cost if wrong: one more term in the camera basis.
- **The front's rounds come from its line.**
  - In a generated world, a bay of the enemy's front line is drawn from `front` within 30 m along the line of the player. The round leaves from 0.4–1.2 m over its crest.
  - In a world without a plan (tests), they come from a line at a set distance, as the reference's rig did: x ± 20 m, z at 0.7–1 of the range, y 0.4–1.2 m over the ground.
  - The aim is the eye plus ±1.6 m sideways and ±0.96 m up (the reference's `incoming_spread_m`).
- **The light intensity** is 40 rounds a minute, single shots. **Heavy** is 500: bursts of 8 at 600 rpm, every fifth a tracer. Both are new tunables in `[front]`, set so light reads as sporadic sniping and heavy pins a man on the step within 6 s at 22 m (the reference's test).
- **The particles** of the reference's view kick (the flash and the brass) wait for milestone 3's effects. This milestone keeps the view kick's state and its muzzle point.

## Review Focus

- **A burst while pinned:** recoil, flinch and the wired sway multiply in the cone together, and the round still leaves inside the stance's maximum cone × 4.2. Pinned in Task 5.
- **Recoil across a reload, a stance change or the periscope:** the climb bleeds off and nothing jumps. Pinned in Task 1.
- **Incoming fire while paused or flying:** the session does not tick, so nothing suppresses. Pinned in Task 6.
- **The front at a map edge, or with no enemy bay within 30 m:** it fires from the nearest bay, or holds fire, with no error. Pinned in Task 4.
- **The camera's roll at a pitch near ±88°:** the basis stays orthonormal, and the frame's motion vectors stay finite. Pinned in Task 5.
