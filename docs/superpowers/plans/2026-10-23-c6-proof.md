**Goal:** Prove sub-project 3 against its done table. Peak combat at every standard view, at dusk and at night, holds the 14 ms GPU budget at 1.5 MP and the 8 ms main thread in Chrome. Safari is measured under the same peak and reported. The load budgets hold, and every earlier test passes.

**Architecture:**
- **One peak-combat driver** (`src/app/peak-combat.ts`), used by Chrome's perf gate through a test hook, and by the check run (`?check=`), which Safari's report reads.
- **Peak combat** is the done table's:
  - a magazine on full auto (refilled, never reloaded, so the fire is sustained);
  - shells landing 20–50 m off at one a second;
  - the front's incoming fire at the heavy rate.
- **Where the player stands:**
  - At the standing views (bay, step, no man's land, dugout, hamlet), the player stands at the view and plays it, with the rifle drawn.
  - At the overview, the camera holds the view's pose while the session runs (*spectating*), and the player fires from the bay below.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WebGPU, Playwright (Chrome), Safari through `npm run safari`.

## Global Constraints

- **The budgets:**
  - GPU ≤ 14 ms at 1.5 MP: the high preset, dynamic resolution off, a 1600 × 1000 canvas at a density of 1. Judged per frame by timestamps (each frame's own time, `gpuLastMs`), at the 95th percentile.
  - Main thread ≤ 8 ms a frame at the 99th percentile, and no frame over 20 ms (row 3's).
- **Peak combat:** full auto with the magazine refilled; a shell a second at 20–50 m, each 2.2–3.4 m; the front `heavy`.
- **The approved look holds.** No look changes in this milestone. A budget that fails is fixed in cost, not in look, or else ruled on and left for the user.
- **Architecture rules hold** (tests/architecture.test.ts). The driver is app code: it may use the session, the renderer's stats and the hooks.
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work stays on `web/world`, and nothing is pushed.
- **Never get past the Mac's lock screen.** If Safari cannot run (locked, or Safari not in front), its run is reported as owed.

## Rulings made in planning

1. **The overview is measured spectating.** The player cannot stand 48 m up. The camera holds the overview's pose and the session runs as if playing. The player fires from the bay's spawn, shells land 20–50 m from them, and the front fires at them, all in the overview's field.
   - Spectating is a test and check facility (`spectate(pose | null)`), not a game mode a visitor can reach.
2. **Shells stay on the map.** A shell whose 20–50 m point falls off the world, or on no ground, is turned by the next 1.9 rad step until it lands somewhere: at most five turns, and after five failures it is skipped and counted. Near the world's edge (the hamlet at the back) a shell must not throw.
3. **The check run's peak.**
   - It is measured at the overview and the bay, at dusk and at night: the two views that bound the cost, the farthest field and the nearest walls. Measuring every view would add about 90 s to a run already near the e2e timeout.
   - The Chrome gate in perf.spec measures all six.
   - Safari reports the four.
   - The check never starts sound (it has no click), so its main thread carries no voices; Chrome's gate does.
4. **Main-thread time** is the frame callback's (the `mainMs` ring). Each frame's GPU time is `gpuLastMs`, taken once per new sample; without timestamps (Safari), the median and the 95th percentile of the submitted work over the same frames.

## Review Focus

- **A shell off the map.** Near the hamlet or the world's edge, the driver's 20–50 m point leaves the map: it turns or skips, and never throws. Pinned in Task 1.
- **State left behind.** After a view's peak, the trigger is let go, the front is off and every shell has landed, so the next view starts calm. Pinned in Task 1.
- **Spectating is not playing.** The HUD and the rifle stay hidden, input still drives nothing, and the camera does not follow the player. Pinned in Task 1.
- **Safari's timer.** Without timestamps, the check's peak is timed by submitted work, and the report says so. Pinned in Task 3.
- **The check's length.** The check with its peak still finishes inside check.spec's timeout. Pinned in Task 3.
